SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

Overview

This application sends SMS notifications only to the account owner/business owner for internal operational alerts. Messages may include AI workflow alerts, real estate lead notifications, offer status updates, system monitoring alerts, and related business notifications.

Mobile Number Use

The mobile phone number used for SMS notifications belongs to the account owner/business owner. The number is used only to deliver internal operational text notifications requested and configured by the account owner.

No Sharing or Selling of Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are not shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on workflow activity, system events, and alerts configured by the account owner.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out

The recipient may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. The account owner may also disable SMS notifications directly within the application or workflow configuration.

Help

For help, the recipient may reply HELP to any message or contact the application administrator.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this SMS Privacy Policy, contact the business owner or application administrator.
