SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

Overview

This application is a personal, single-user notification system. It sends SMS notifications only to the account owner at the mobile phone number personally provided and configured by the account owner. Messages may include real estate lead notifications, AI-generated property analysis, underwriting results, offer recommendations, workflow notifications, and system alerts.

Mobile Number Use

The mobile phone number used for SMS notifications belongs to the account owner. The number is used only to deliver notifications requested and configured by the account owner.

Consent to Receive SMS Messages

The account owner consents to receive SMS notifications by entering and saving their own mobile phone number in the application's SMS notification settings and enabling SMS notifications. SMS notifications may be disabled at any time.

No Sharing or Selling of Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are not shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on real estate lead activity, workflow activity, system events, and alerts configured by the account owner.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out

The recipient may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. The account owner may also disable SMS notifications directly within the application or workflow configuration.

Help

For help, reply HELP to any message.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the mobile phone number and related notification configuration from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this SMS Privacy Policy, contact the application administrator through the same communication channel used to access or configure the application.
