SMS Program Description

This SMS program is a personal, single-user notification system. It provides automated notifications only to the account owner at the mobile phone number personally provided and configured by the account owner. Messages may include real estate lead notifications, AI-generated property analysis, underwriting results, offer recommendations, workflow notifications, and system alerts.

Consent to Receive Messages

By entering and saving their own mobile phone number in the application's SMS notification settings and enabling SMS notifications, the account owner consents to receive automated text messages at that mobile phone number. No third-party customers or external users receive messages through this SMS program.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on real estate lead activity, workflow activity, system events, and alert settings configured by the account owner.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out Instructions

The recipient may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, the recipient will no longer receive SMS notifications unless they opt in again or re-enable SMS notifications.

Help Instructions
